Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about NexusWeave

Have questions about security keys, timer precision, or workspace setup? Explore our quick answers below.

How do organization security keys and role permissions work? +
Organizations are created by an Admin who sets a secret Organization Key. Employees must select the organization and enter the key to join. Admins hold full management rights over team projects, while Employees are granted execution rights.
Why does the Pomodoro focus timer remain accurate in background tabs? +
Standard JavaScript timers drift when browsers throttle inactive background tabs. NexusWeave computes remaining time from absolute target timestamps (`Date.now() + duration`), guaranteeing zero drift regardless of tab switching.
How does the AI milestone generator breakdown projects? +
When you enter a high-level project goal, NexusWeave's AI engine parses your prompt into structured milestones, prioritizing task deliverables and estimating completion timelines automatically.
Can I switch between Personal and Organization Workspaces? +
Yes! You can operate an isolated Personal Workspace for individual task queues and Pomodoro sprints, or toggle to your Organization scope for team sprint boards and WebSocket real-time messaging.
